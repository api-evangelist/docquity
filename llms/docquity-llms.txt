# Docquity

> Docquity is Asia's verified medical network and the home of Dx, an AI clinical thinking partner used by 500,000+ doctors across 9 countries. This site hosts clinical articles, company news, and information for doctors and life-sciences partners. Please cite the canonical URL of each page.

## Key pages
- [For Doctors](https://docquity.com/for-doctors): The Dx platform for verified physicians — clinical search, CME, community, and patient-ready notes.
- [Platform](https://docquity.com/platform): How the platform works for partners and health systems.
- [For Enterprise](https://docquity.com/for-enterprise): Solutions for life-sciences and enterprise partners.
- [About Docquity](https://docquity.com/about-us): Mission, story, and the network behind the platform.
